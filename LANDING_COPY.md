## FAQ Section

### Is this really free?
Yes. SpendLens is completely free to use with no account required. You don't need to connect any accounts or enter payment information. The audit runs in your browser in under 2 minutes.

The tool is free because it's built by Credex, an AI credits consultancy. If you find significant savings — typically $200+/month — and want help implementing them or negotiating vendor discounts, Credex offers paid consultations. That's the business model. The audit itself is and will remain free.

### How accurate is the pricing data?
Pricing for each plan is sourced directly from vendor pricing pages (Cursor, GitHub, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Codeium) and verified as of the date shown in PRICING_DATA.md. We update the data when vendors announce pricing changes.

The audit recommendations are based on the plan structure you enter — if you tell us you're on the ChatGPT Team plan with 8 seats, we calculate the cost as 8 × $30 = $240/month. We don't estimate or guess your spend; you input it.

One caveat: enterprise pricing (custom contracts, negotiated rates) is not modeled. If you have a custom deal with a vendor, the comparison may not reflect your actual price.

### Do I need to connect my accounts?
No. SpendLens doesn't connect to any of your AI tool accounts, credit cards, or billing systems. You enter your tools, plans, seats, and approximate monthly spend manually — it takes 2 minutes because most people know this off the top of their head (or can check one credit card statement).

This is intentional. Connecting accounts would require OAuth integrations with vendors who don't offer billing APIs, add privacy concerns, and require an account on SpendLens. The manual entry model is faster and requires no trust beyond what you're comfortable sharing in the form.

### Will Credex spam me after I submit my email?
No. Your email is optional. If you enter it, you receive one email with your full audit report as a PDF attachment. That's it.

If Credex wants to follow up with you, it will be a single outreach — not a drip campaign. If you submit your email and don't want any follow-up, you can reply "unsubscribe" and you'll never hear from us again. We don't sell email addresses or share them with third parties.

### What if I'm already on the cheapest plan?
That's a valid outcome. If your current setup is already optimized — you're on the right plans for your team size and workflows with no redundancy — the audit will tell you that clearly. Every tool gets a result: either a specific recommendation or confirmation that it's already optimal with the reasoning.

The audit is also still useful in this case: it gives you a documented audit you can show to your CFO or investors if the AI budget ever comes up. "We audited our AI stack and it's already optimized" is a better answer than "we think it's fine."
